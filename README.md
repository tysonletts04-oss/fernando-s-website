# Fernando's Peruvian Rotisserie — Website

A website project for **Fernando's Peruvian Rotisserie**, a family-run Peruvian
rotisserie and burger joint in North Avalon, on Sydney's Northern Beaches.

> _"Best chicken on the northern beaches! Juicy, tasty and the skin is
> delicious."_ — Customer review

---

## About the business

Fernando's Peruvian Rotisserie is a colourful, dog-friendly neighbourhood
eatery run by a **father-and-son team**. It serves Peruvian-inspired comfort
food — the signature being **Pollo a la Brasa** (Peruvian-style charcoal
rotisserie chicken) — alongside loaded bacon & egg rolls, gourmet burgers,
burritos, salchipapas, and house-made sauces like chimichurri, huancaína, and
ají amarillo. It doubles as a morning coffee-and-breakfast spot and a
lunch/dinner takeaway, with casual outdoor seating that welcomes dogs.

Fernando himself is described by regulars as a proud, friendly host who clearly
loves his food — and the community treats it as a local favourite.

## Website goals

1. **Show up in local search** — when someone Googles "Peruvian chicken Avalon"
   or "best rotisserie Northern Beaches," Fernando's should appear.
2. **Make it effortless to order** — prominent phone tap-to-call and links to
   Uber Eats / DoorDash.
3. **Make people hungry** — big, warm photography of the chicken, rolls & burgers.
4. **Answer the basics fast** — hours, address, map, and menu above the fold.

---

## Proposed site structure

- **Home** — hero shot of the rotisserie chicken, tagline, hours, "Order Now"
  and "Call" buttons, location map.
- **Menu** — full menu by category with prices and dietary notes.
- **About** — the father-son story, Peruvian heritage, the community vibe.
- **Gallery** — food + venue photography (and the dog-friendly outdoor area).
- **Find Us / Contact** — embedded map, address, phone, hours, parking notes,
  social links.
- **Order Online** — buttons/links out to Uber Eats and DoorDash.

### Suggested components
- Sticky header with **"Call"** and **"Order Online"** CTAs.
- Click-to-call (`tel:+61289262621`) and click-for-directions links.
- Open/closed status indicator driven by trading hours.
- Mobile-first, fast-loading (most visitors will be on phones, on the go).

---

## Suggested tech stack

- Static site for speed & low cost — plain HTML/CSS/JS, **Astro**, or **Next.js**.
- Tailwind CSS for styling.
- Deploy on Netlify / Vercel / GitHub Pages.
- **Local SEO:** add `LocalBusiness` / `Restaurant` schema.org JSON-LD with the
  NAP, hours, geo, and menu; register/optimise the Google Business Profile.

### Branding direction
- **Vibe:** warm, sunny, coastal-casual with Peruvian colour accents.
- **Palette:** charcoal/roast browns + warm reds/oranges + a fresh green
  (chimichurri) accent.
- **Tone:** friendly, local, proud, unpretentious.
