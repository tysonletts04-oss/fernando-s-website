A father, a son & a rotisserie

Fernando's is a colourful little corner of Peru in North Avalon, run by a father-and-son team who take genuine pride in every plate. The chicken is marinated, spun low over charcoal and served with crispy skin and house-made sauces: chimichurri, huancaína and ají amarillo.

It's the kind of place where the organic Kingdom Roasters coffee is on by 6am, the dog's welcome at your feet, and Fernando might just tell you himself why his pollo is the best on the beaches.
